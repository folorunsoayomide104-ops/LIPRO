Cookie Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy explains what cookies are, which ones LIPRO ACADEMY uses, and how you can manage them. It forms part of our Privacy Policy.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by a website. They help the site remember you, your preferences and how you interact with it. They can be set by the site itself (“first-party”) or by services we use (“third-party”).

2. How we use cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in, remember your settings, and understand how the platform is used so we can improve it. We also use local storage for the same purposes where appropriate.

3. Types of cookies we use

TypePurposeCan it be disabled?
Essential Required for the platform to work — signing in, keeping your session secure, and maintaining your study data. No — the platform won’t function without them.
Preferences Remember your university, department, theme and other choices so you don’t re-enter them. Yes — some personalisation will be lost.
Analytics Help us understand aggregate usage — pages visited, study time and features used — to improve the product. Yes — usage statistics become less detailed.

4. Managing cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block all cookies, block third-party cookies, or delete cookies on exit. Note that disabling essential cookies may stop LIPRO ACADEMY from working correctly.

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data.
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data.
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions.

5. Third-party services

Services we rely on — such as secure payment providers and analytics — may set their own cookies. We do not control those cookies, and you should review the respective providers’ policies for more information.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
