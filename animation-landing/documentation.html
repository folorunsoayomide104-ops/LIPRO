Platform guide

A practical walkthrough of every part of LIPRO ACADEMY, from your first login to your first exam simulation. Written for real students on real Nigerian campuses.

1. Getting started

Create a free account and confirm your email. Then complete your academic profile: university, faculty and department. LIPRO ACADEMY uses this to build your course list and question bank around your syllabus.

2. Courses & materials

Every course you register for appears under Courses with four tabs:

  • Course Notes — organised, easy-to-read study notes.
  • Course PDFs — upload and store lecture PDFs in one place.
  • Course Outlines — your syllabus mapped out topic by topic.
  • Videos — supplementary video lessons for difficult topics.

3. LIPRO AI

LIPRO AI is your on-demand tutor. Open the AI assistant from any course and you can:

  • Chat — ask questions about any topic in your syllabus.
  • Generate quizzes — turn a chapter into practice questions instantly.
  • Deep research — get a well-organised, cited deep-dive on complex topics.

The Free plan includes basic LIPRO AI. Premium includes advanced AI, and Ultimate unlocks unlimited AI usage, PDF intelligence and deep research.

4. PDF Intelligence

Upload a lecture PDF and LIPRO AI extracts the key points automatically. From a single upload you can generate summaries, revision guides and flashcards — so hours of reading become minutes of studying.

6. Study Planner

Set daily and weekly study goals and LIPRO ACADEMY schedules them around your lectures. The exam countdown keeps every deadline in view, and notifications remind you when it’s time to study.

7. Analytics

  • Performance Tracking — scores over time, per course and per topic.
  • Study Time — how many hours you’ve put in, and where.
  • Progress Reports — weekly summaries of what improved.
  • Weak Topic Detection — LIPRO flags the topics costing you marks.

8. Community & Wallet

Climb the department, faculty and university leaderboards, unlock achievements, and earn rewards. The Wallet handles your subscription, balance and payment history — and sends you alerts on course updates, exam reminders and subscription renewals.

10. Study tips

  • Use Practice Mode while you learn, and save Exam Mode for the week before your test.
  • Upload PDFs the day you get them — small, regular study beats cramming every time.
  • Watch your Weak Topic Detection report weekly and attack the topics it flags.
