FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is LIPRO AI? +
LIPRO AI is your intelligent academic assistant. It answers questions about your courses, generates quizzes and practice questions, analyzes your PDFs, and performs deep research — all tailored to your university, faculty and department.
How does the CBT Engine work? +
The CBT Engine simulates real computer-based tests. Practice Mode helps you learn with instant feedback, while Exam Mode mirrors a real timed exam with mixed question types and instant results when you finish.
Which universities are supported? +
We currently support UNIOSUN, UNILAG, UI, OAU, FUTA, LASU and UNILORIN — and we're expanding to all Nigerian universities. If yours isn't listed, contact us and we'll add it.
Can I upload PDFs? +
Yes. Upload your course PDFs and LIPRO AI will analyze them, extract key points, and generate summaries, revision guides, flashcards and practice questions from the content.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Absolutely. Premium and Ultimate subscriptions can be cancelled anytime from your Wallet — no questions asked. You keep access until the end of your current billing period.
Is my data secure? +
Yes. Your study data, documents and payment information are encrypted and stored securely. We never share your personal information with third parties.
How do I upgrade? +
Head to the Pricing section, choose Premium or Ultimate, and follow the prompts. Payment is handled securely through your Wallet, and you can upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime.
