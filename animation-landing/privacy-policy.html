Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 July 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information LIPRO ACADEMY (“we”, “us” or “our”) collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the platform you agree to the practices described below.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — name, email address, password, and your academic details (university, faculty, department).
  • Study content — course notes, uploaded PDFs, study plans and the study materials you create.
  • Payment information — handled by secure payment providers. We store a record of your transactions in your Wallet but never your full card details.
  • Messages — anything you send us through support channels.

Information collected automatically

  • Device type, browser, and operating system.
  • Usage data such as pages visited, study time, quiz attempts and feature usage.
  • Log data including IP address, access times and referring pages.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate the platform and deliver your courses, AI assistance, CBT tests and analytics.
  • To personalise content, questions and study plans to your university, faculty and department.
  • To process subscriptions and payments, and send billing and service notifications.
  • To improve our product, respond to support requests, and keep the platform secure.
  • To send exam reminders, course updates and subscription alerts you have opted into.

3. Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and understand how the platform is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings. See our Cookie Policy for details.

4. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with:

  • Service providers — payment processors, hosting and analytics providers that help us run the platform, under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal authorities — where required by law, or to protect our rights, safety and property.

5. How we protect your data

We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest, restrict access to personal data to authorised staff only, and review our security practices regularly. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we work hard to keep your data safe.

6. Data retention

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. You may request deletion at any time.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at liproacademy96@gmail.com.

8. Children’s privacy

LIPRO ACADEMY is intended for university students and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and notify you through the platform where appropriate.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Reach us at liproacademy96@gmail.com or on WhatsApp.
