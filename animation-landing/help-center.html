Help Center

How can we help you?

Everything you need to get the most out of LIPRO ACADEMY — from creating your account to mastering the CBT Engine. If you can’t find an answer here, our support team is one message away.

How do I create an account?

Click Get Started Free on the homepage, enter your name, email and a password, then verify your email. No card is required for the Free plan.

How do I select my university, faculty and department?

After sign-up, go to your Profile and pick your university, faculty and department. LIPRO ACADEMY then tailors your courses, questions and study materials to your exact programme.

My university isn’t listed yet. What now?

We’re adding Nigerian universities all the time. Message us on WhatsApp with your university name and we’ll prioritise it.

What is LIPRO AI and how do I use it?

LIPRO AI is your academic assistant. Open any course and use the chat to ask questions, generate practice questions or get a deep-dive on a topic. On paid plans you get unlimited AI usage.

How do I upload a PDF?

Open your course, go to Course PDFs and upload the file. LIPRO AI will analyze it and generate summaries, revision guides, flashcards and questions from the content.

How does the CBT Engine work?

Practice Mode gives instant feedback as you answer so you can learn as you go. Exam Mode is a timed, real-exam simulation with mixed question types and instant results when you finish.

How do I upgrade or cancel my subscription?

Go to Wallet > Subscription Management. You can upgrade, downgrade or cancel anytime. If you cancel, you keep access until the end of your current billing period.

How do I change my university or department?

Open Profile > Academic Details and update your selection. Your courses and materials will update to match your new programme.

Still stuck?

Reach us directly and we’ll help you sort it out — usually within a few hours.
